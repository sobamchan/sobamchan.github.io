---
title: GenGO MCP - Allow agents to search over *ACL conference papers
updated: 2026-06-08
---

To enable more interactive and autonomous literature search over *ACL papers, I wrote [a little MCP server](https://github.com/sobamchan/gengomcp) that grants an access to my GenGO paper database.

Conference proceedings just keep getting bigger and bigger. To catch up, we need more efficiency in literature search, while I still enjoy doing "go through all paper titles in proceedings", I wrote this 
MCP server as a little experiment.

While I still prefer manual search in most cases, it is fun to generate a report on more broad topics such as [ACL 2025 → 2026 Research Topic Trend Analysis](https://sotaro.io/info/gengomcp/acl_2025_2026_trend_analysis).
 
Find some example generated literature search reports [here](https://sotaro.io/info/gengomcp/).
